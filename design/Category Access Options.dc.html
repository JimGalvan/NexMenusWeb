My take
A is the most discoverable and self-explanatory — a labeled “Categories · Manage” reads clearly and gives the section a title. B keeps it compact and always-visible by pinning an “Edit” button so it never scrolls away. C is best if you expect more menu-level actions (reorder, duplicate, share) — it’s a home for all of them. D is the lightest touch — quick rename/delete inline — but reorder and longer names get cramped on a phone. I’d go with A, or C if you’re planning more per-menu actions soon.
