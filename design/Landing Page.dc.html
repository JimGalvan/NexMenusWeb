How it works

From sign-up to first scan in three steps

1
Build your menu
Add items, prices, descriptions and photos. Organize them into categories — it takes minutes, no design skills needed.
2
Get your QR code
We generate a QR code and a shareable link instantly. Print it for tables, your window, or flyers — or share on social.
3
Diners scan & browse
Guests see your live, photo-forward menu instantly. Sold out tonight? Hide an item or mark it — it updates the moment you tap save.
