Common questions

How do I edit my menu? Log in and open your menu from My menus — prices, items, photos, hours, and styling are all in the editor, and your public page and printable PDF stay in sync automatically.

Where is my QR code? Every menu has a share link and QR code in the app under QR & Share.

I created a menu in ChatGPT — how do I keep it? Open the claim link from your chat conversation and sign in or create a free account. Unclaimed drafts expire about 72 hours after creation. If your claim link stopped working or you lost it, email us and we'll help.

How do I delete my account or data? Email us at contact@nexmenus.com and we'll take care of it — see our Privacy Policy for details on your rights.
