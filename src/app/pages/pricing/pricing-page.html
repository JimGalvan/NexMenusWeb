Before you upgrade

Can I start free and upgrade later?
Yes, and that's the point. Nothing is time-limited — the free plan doesn't expire, and you can upgrade the day you need a second menu.
I publish menus for lots of different businesses. Does Pro cover that?
Pro covers one business — every location, brand and service you run yourself. If you are publishing menus for places you don't own, like a local directory, a listing site or an agency working for clients, that's a partner plan rather than Pro. Tell us what you're building and we'll put together something that fits.
What happens to my menu if I stop paying?
Nothing disappears. Every menu you've made stays live, keeps its link, and stays editable. You go back to the free plan's one-menu allowance for anything new, and the NexMenus badge returns.
How do I actually pay?
Right now, personally. You tell us you want Pro, we email you a payment link, and we switch your account over. We're small enough that we'd rather talk to our first customers than build a checkout.
Do I need a credit card to start?
No. The free plan never asks for one.
