FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I print my menu?
Yes, on every plan. NexMenus generates a print-ready PDF of your menu — a clean, ink-friendly layout for tables, takeout counters, events, laminating, or guests who prefer paper. It always matches your digital menu. Pro removes the small NexMenus line from the printed page.
Is NexMenus free to use?
Yes. The free plan includes one menu with unlimited items, photos, a public link, a QR code and a printable menu — no credit card. Pro is $9/month and adds unlimited menus, your own menu link, and removes the NexMenus badge from your public and printed menus. See what's included.
Can I make a QR code menu with NexMenus?
Yes. Every menu gets a public link and QR code that guests can scan from table tents, printed signs, your website, or social media.
Do guests need to download an app?
No. Guests open your menu from a normal web link or QR code in their phone's browser — nothing to install.
Can I update prices and sold-out items?
Yes. Update prices, descriptions, photos, and sold-out items from one place — even from your phone — and your printable and digital menus stay aligned.
Can I add photos to my menu?
Yes. NexMenus is designed for photo-forward menus, so you can show dishes visually and help guests decide what to order.
Who is NexMenus for?
Restaurants, cafes, bakeries, food trucks, bars, caterers, pop-ups, and any food business that needs a simple menu it can keep current — in print and online.
