1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us. When you create an account, we collect information such as your name, email address, password, and business details (for example, your restaurant name, address, phone number, and hours). When you build a menu, we collect the menu content you create, including item names, descriptions, prices, categories, and photos.

Payment information. If you purchase a paid subscription, payment is processed by third-party payment processors. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

Information collected automatically. When you use the Services, we automatically collect certain technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and the dates and times of access. This information is used for security, analytics, and improving the Services.

Communications. If you contact us by email or through the Services, we keep a record of that correspondence.

3. Public Menu Content

Menus you publish through NexMenus are public by design. Content you choose to include on a published menu page — such as your business name, address, phone number, hours, menu items, prices, descriptions, and photos — is visible to anyone with the link and may be indexed by search engines. Do not include information on a public menu that you do not want to be public.

4. Menu Drafts Created Through ChatGPT

You can start a menu draft through the NexMenus plugin in ChatGPT without creating a NexMenus account. When you do, we collect the business and menu information you choose to provide in that conversation — such as your business name, menu items, prices, descriptions, hours, address, and contact details — so we can build and store your draft. For security and abuse prevention, we also process your IP address in a hashed (one-way encoded) form to apply rate limits; we do not store the raw IP address with your draft, and we do not receive your ChatGPT account identity.

Drafts are private: they are reachable only through unguessable links, are not published, and are excluded from search engines and from public areas of the Services. Unclaimed drafts become inaccessible automatically (currently about 72 hours after creation), and their access credentials are revoked. The associated draft records and credentials are then permanently deleted by scheduled cleanup after an additional retention period, currently up to seven days. If you claim a draft by signing in or creating an account, the draft becomes part of your account and the rest of this Privacy Policy applies to it.

Your conversation with ChatGPT itself is processed by OpenAI under OpenAI's privacy policy; we only receive the specific information the plugin sends to NexMenus to build your draft. To request earlier deletion of an unclaimed draft, contact us at contact@nexmenus.com.

5. AI Menu Builder

The Services include an AI assistant that builds or edits a menu through chat, on our website and inside your account. When you use it, we process the messages you send and the menu information in the conversation — such as your business name, menu items, prices, descriptions, hours, address, and contact details — to generate replies and to make the changes you ask for. Please share only information about your business and menu.

AI providers. To generate replies, your messages and the related menu content are sent to the third-party AI model providers we use. They process this information on our behalf to provide the assistant.

Using the assistant without an account. Drafts you build without an account follow the rules in "Menu Drafts Created Through ChatGPT" above: we process your IP address only in hashed form, drafts are private, and unclaimed drafts expire and are deleted on the same schedule. The conversation itself is kept only as long as needed to continue it, and is deleted within seven days.

Using the assistant in your account. If you are signed in, your conversation history is saved to your account so you can review it later, and it is retained and deleted with your account as described in "Data Retention." Changes you ask the assistant to make to a published menu take effect immediately.

Quality monitoring. To find and fix mistakes, we use an AI monitoring service, LangSmith (provided by LangChain, Inc.), which may record a sample of builder conversations, including messages and menu content. We review these records only to improve the assistant, and the service keeps them for a limited period. We may keep selected conversations, with contact details removed, as test examples to check future versions of the assistant.
